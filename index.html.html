Aviation Safety Disclaimer

Iran AeroWX is a situational awareness, decoding, and training companion. Default bundled bulletins are simulated reference data for offline demonstration until you paste official bulletins from IRIMO or connect a verified live proxy. Pilots in command must always obtain and verify official pre-flight meteorological briefings and NOTAMs through IRIMO / Iran Airports & Air Navigation Company (IAC) AIS channels prior to flight.
